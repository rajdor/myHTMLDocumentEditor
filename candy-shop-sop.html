Refilling the pick and mix wall

Standard operating procedure for shop staff. Version 1.0

Every delivery of loose sweets goes through four steps before it reaches the wall: receive, check allergens, rotate and refill, then label and record. The allergen check is the step that must never be skipped.

Purpose and scope

Why this procedure exists and where it applies.

This procedure keeps the pick and mix wall full, fresh and safe. It applies to every loose product sold from a bin on the wall, from the moment a delivery arrives until the refill is recorded.

It does not cover pre-packed products on the shelves, or the chocolate counter, which has its own procedure.

Before you start

Have everything to hand before you open a delivery box.

Equipment

  • A clean scoop and tongs for each bin you will refill
  • Food safe gloves
  • The allergen matrix and the refill log
  • The label printer, loaded with bin labels

Checks

  • CHK-01 Hands washed and gloves on
  • CHK-02 Scoops and tongs are visibly clean and dry
  • CHK-03 The quarantine shelf in the back room is empty and labelled

Procedure

Four steps, always in this order.

Figure 1. The four steps, and where a product goes when its label and the matrix disagree.

Step 1: Receive

  1. R-01 Check each box against the order: product, quantity and best before date.
  2. R-02 Refuse any box that is damaged, open or past its date.
  3. R-03 Ask the shift lead to sign the delivery docket.

Step 2: Check allergens

  1. C-01 Read the allergen statement on the supplier label.
  2. C-02 Find the product in the allergen matrix and compare its allergen profile with the label.
  3. C-03 Ask the shift lead to confirm the match before the box leaves the back room.

If the label and the matrix disagree

Stop. Put the box on the quarantine shelf, tell the shop manager, and do not refill the bin until the matrix has been updated.

Step 3: Rotate and refill

  1. F-01 Lift the bin liner and move the older sweets to the front.
  2. F-02 Pour the new stock in behind the old, using the scoop for that bin only.
  3. F-03 Wipe the lid and the front of the bin.

Step 4: Label and record

  1. L-01 Print a new bin label and replace the old one.
  2. L-02 Fill in the refill log: bin, product, best before date, your initials and the time.

Common questions

A customer asks whether a sweet contains nuts. What do I say?
Read the bin label to them and offer to show the supplier label. Never answer from memory.
A scoop was put back in the wrong bin. What now?
Treat it as allergen cross-contact. Remove both scoops for washing and tell the shift lead, who decides whether the bin must be emptied.
The label printer is not working.
Write the label by hand in the same format, and note it in the refill log.
