{{ic:notebook}}Notebook
  • The Notebook tab at the left edge shows the explorer; the arrows at the top of the panel hide it. Choose a folder: its folders are sections and its files are pages. Chrome and Edge only; the folder is remembered and reconnected with one click.
  • Click a page to open it. If the open page has unsaved changes you are asked to save, discard or cancel.
  • A new page is created in the selected folder. Its file is made at the first Save and named after the title (the first H1); with no title it is Untitled and the first number free in its folder. After that only Rename changes the name.
  • Right-click for New page, New folder, Rename and Move to trash. Drag a page or folder onto a folder to move it. F2 renames and Delete moves to trash.
  • Sort: the Sort button at the top of the explorer sorts the whole notebook by name, modified date, size or type, ascending or descending. Folders always come first. To sort one folder its own way, right-click it and choose Sort this folder. Setting the notebook sort again clears the folders' own sorts.
  • Favourites: point at a page and click its star, or right-click and choose Add to favourites. Favourite pages are listed at the top of the explorer; click one to open it, or its star to take it out. A favourite follows its page when you rename or move it here. One whose page has gone is greyed.
  • Deleted items go to the .trash folder, keeping their path. Right-click an item there to restore it, or drag it out.
  • History: a full copy of a page is kept in the .history folder each time you save over an earlier version, and every 5 minutes while the page has unsaved changes. Right-click a page and choose Page history to restore a copy. If the editor closed with unsaved changes, opening the page offers to recover them.
  • The line at the foot of the explorer shows how much space history and trash take. Click it to review and clean up.
  • Markdown files (.md) open in this editor and are saved back as Markdown. Toolbar commands for things Markdown cannot hold, such as boxes, diagrams and notes, are switched off while one is open. It is shown plain, not in the Fine Line page style. Pictures stored in the notebook folder are shown. The Source button in the bar under the toolbar shows the file's Markdown source, and switches back; unsaved changes go with it.
  • Text, data and code files (JSON, XML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, SQL, YAML, shell, PowerShell, batch and plain text) open in a text editor, coloured by the kind of file, and are saved as themselves, with the encoding and line endings they had. Find is Ctrl+F. Tab and Enter indent the way the file already does. Print prints the whole file with line numbers.
  • Other HTML files open as an import and save as a new page; the original file is left as it is. Files the editor cannot open are greyed; right-click to open one in a new browser tab. For kinds the browser cannot show, such as Word, Excel and PowerPoint files and archives, the item is there but switched off.
  • In other browsers the explorer is read-only and Save downloads the page.
